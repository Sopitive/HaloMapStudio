Camera & navigation

The camera is a free-flying first-person camera. All movement keys work while the right mouse button is held over the viewport. While you fly the OS cursor is hidden and captured (locked in place on Windows and Wayland, confined to the window on X11), so it cannot drift onto the side panels or out of the window mid-flight; releasing the button gives it back. The same capture applies while a grab / rotate transform is active.

To copy a view into a script or a headless render, run camera get in the script console: it prints the position plus yaw and pitch in degrees, the units camera to / camera lookat and the headless HMS_CAM use. (The Advanced (diagnostics) section of the Settings window also shows the pose, but with the angles in radians.)

Framing and jumping

  • F (with nothing else held) or View ▸ Frame selected object flies the camera to the selected object. While the right button is held F is the fly-down key instead.
  • Double-clicking an object in the left panel's object list flies to it.
  • Opening a variant places the camera at eye height on an initial spawn point, looking the way the player would spawn.
  • The base map's own built-in spawn markers are hidden by default; View ▸ Show map spawn points draws them (they cannot be edited or saved — your placed spawn points always show).
  • The Light bake map window has per-result Camera buttons that jump to a found spot.

Camera stand-off (collision)

In Settings ▸ Lighting & rendering… ▸ Camera, render & display, tick Camera stand-off and set a distance in world units (default 2.0). The camera then keeps at least that distance from level geometry and objects while you fly, so you cannot end up inside a wall. Push camera out applies it once to the current position; Measure reports the nearest surface. Scripts expose the same solver as camera standoff and camera clearance.

Screenshots

F12, the 📷 F12 button or View ▸ Screenshot saves the viewport to captures/viewer_last.png next to the executable (each shot overwrites the previous one; the camera pose is written alongside as viewer_last.cam). For fixed-size or batch screenshots use the headless renderer or the script screenshot command.

Camera commands in scripts

The scripting reference has the full list. In short:

camera to 40 -70 104.5        # position
camera lookat 40 -260 101.5   # aim at a point
camera nudge 0 0 5            # move by a delta
camera orbit 0xD8000001 12 45 -25   # 12 wu from an object, yaw 45°, pitch -25°
camera spawn                  # the variant's spawn camera
camera frame                  # frame the placed objects
camera get                    # print pos / yaw / pitch / fly speed
camera standoff 2             # push out of nearby geometry
camera clearance              # report nearest surface
