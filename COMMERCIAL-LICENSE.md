Halo Map Studio is free for **noncommercial** use under the PolyForm

commercial license from the copyright holder.

## What needs a commercial license

You need a commercial license if you, or the organization you work for, use
Halo Map Studio (the application, its source code, or works derived from it) in
a way that is intended for, or directed toward, commercial advantage or
monetary compensation. Examples:

* Selling, renting, or charging a fee for access to the software or a modified
  version of it, including as part of another product or a hosted service.
* Using the software as part of a paid service (map-making commissions, paid
  Forge design, paid modding services, tooling sold to other creators).
* Using the software inside a for-profit company for any business purpose,
  including internal tooling, content production, or evaluation for a
  commercial project.
* Bundling the code or the native library into a commercial product, even if
  the Halo Map Studio parts themselves are not charged for.
* Monetized distribution (paywalled downloads, "premium" builds, sponsored
  forks) of the software or a derivative.

Noncommercial use that stays free (per the PolyForm terms): personal projects,
hobby map-making, private entertainment, study, research and experiments for
public knowledge, and use by charities, schools, public research bodies and
government institutions.

Grey areas (for example ad-supported videos that *feature* maps made with the
tool, or free tools that link to a donation page) are not automatically
commercial use of the software; if in doubt, ask.

## How to obtain one

Commercial licenses are granted individually by the copyright holder and are
not available under a fixed public price list.

1. Contact the copyright holder (Sopitive) via the project's repository issue
   tracker or the contact listed on the project page, describing:
   * who the licensee would be (person or company),
   * what the software will be used for,
   * whether the source code will be modified or redistributed,
   * expected scale (users, seats, deployments).
2. A written commercial license agreement is then issued covering scope, term,
   fee and support. The commercial license does not affect the noncommercial
   terms for anyone else.

Until a written commercial license is in place, the PolyForm Noncommercial
License 1.0.0 is the only license you have to this software, and commercial
use is not permitted.

## Third-party components

The commercial license covers only the code owned by the copyright holder.
Third-party components bundled with the software (see
`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`) remain under their own permissive licenses, which
already allow commercial use with attribution.
