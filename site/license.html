Licence & commercial use

Halo Map Studio is licensed under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. It is free for noncommercial use; any commercial use requires a separate commercial licence from the copyright holder.

The authoritative texts ship with every download and in the source repository: LICENSE.md (the full PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 text with the required notice), COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md and THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md. This page is a summary and is not a substitute for them.

What you may do for free

Use, study, modify and share the software for any noncommercial purpose, as long as the licence notice stays with it. Per the PolyForm terms this includes personal projects, hobby map-making, private entertainment, study, research and experiments for public knowledge, and use by charities, schools, public research bodies and government institutions.

Required notice: Copyright (c) 2026 Sopitive — Halo Map Studio. Noncommercial use only; commercial use requires a license from the copyright holder (see COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md).

What needs a commercial licence

Any use of the application, its source code or a derivative that is intended for, or directed toward, commercial advantage or monetary compensation. Examples from COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md:

  • Selling, renting or charging for access to the software or a modified version, including as part of another product or a hosted service.
  • Using it in a paid service — map-making commissions, paid Forge design, paid modding services, tooling sold to other creators.
  • Using it inside a for-profit company for any business purpose, including internal tooling, content production or evaluation for a commercial project.
  • Bundling the code or the native library into a commercial product, even if the Halo Map Studio parts are free.
  • Monetised distribution — paywalled downloads, "premium" builds, sponsored forks.

Grey areas — for example ad-supported videos that feature maps made with the tool, or free tools that link to a donation page — are not automatically commercial use of the software. If in doubt, ask.

How to obtain a commercial licence

Commercial licences are granted individually; there is no fixed public price list.

  1. Contact the copyright holder (Sopitive) through the project's repository issue tracker or the contact listed on the project page, describing who the licensee would be, what the software will be used for, whether the source will be modified or redistributed, and the expected scale (users, seats, deployments).
  2. A written commercial licence agreement is then issued covering scope, term, fee and support. It does not change the noncommercial terms for anyone else.

Until a written commercial licence is in place, the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 is the only licence you have, and commercial use is not permitted.

Third-party components

Libraries bundled with the software keep their own permissive licences (listed in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md); those already allow commercial use with attribution. The commercial licence covers only the code owned by the copyright holder.
